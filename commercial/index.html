Restaurant Services

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as lukewarm water at the dish machine and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a critical issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and staging temporary hot water where possible.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Grapevine Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.
